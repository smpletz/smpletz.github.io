I found UX by following the part of technology that interested me most: understanding why people struggle, what they actually need, and how a team can respond.

My background crosses informatics, computer science, data science, game design, and hands-on development. That range helps me work comfortably between research, design, engineering, and product conversations (not just hand off findings at the end).

I’m currently pursuing an M.S. in Human–Computer Interaction at Indiana University, graduating in Spring 2028. I’m looking for opportunities where research has a real voice in product direction while remaining close to the people who design and build it.
