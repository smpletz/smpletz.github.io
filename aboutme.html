About Me

Academics

I am a recent graduate from Indiana University Bloomington, where I studied a variety of fields under the programming spectrum. I came to IU as a computer science student with a general idea of the kind of work I wanted to do, but quickly found what areas I’m most passionate about. In my sophomore year I took my first class on mobile app development and found myself enjoying the work I was doing, especially with visual elements. At the same time, I was starting on my game design minor, which was a field I have always been interested in contributing to. While continuing on with my game design minor, I switched my degree path to Informatics to focus on web and app development, since many of the courses offered at IU over the subject weren’t under the computer science department. Informatics changed my view on where my talents shine and where I can make an impact within the spaces I was already a part of. After getting a chance to really work with Figma, I developed a deeper interest in the design space, leading me to take "Interactive Design Practice," a course that allowed me to get a taste of what designers at companies like Salesforce, Adobe, and ESPN do every day. After some discussion with the professor and former head of IU’s HCI program, I decided to pursue HCI further through IU’s graduate program. I anticipate graduating from the program in Spring of 2028 to continue designing futures.

Career

After graduating, I plan to make full use of the skills I’ve practiced in college. Ideally, I’ll work with either a focus on design in the fields of web/app or game development. Outside of the work I will do for my official career, I plan to make use of my other talents on my own time. The skills I have gained in college will allow me to work on my own and complete full projects in the fields that interest me personally. I aim to never stop learning and give back to the communities that made me who I am today, both in and out of my career. At the moment, this looks like working with IU’s Product Management Club to make a real difference in the local communities, particularly in the interest of finding roommates and housing.

Employment

I currently work with IU’s Recreational Sports department to contribute further to a diverse community that has contributed so much to who I am today. I have also spent time as a teaching assistant for mobile app development in iOS, which allowed me to maintain practice in the development side of the field while I continued my studies on design. This also allowed me to work closely with others interested in becoming developers and learn from them. I will also be a teaching assistant in the Fall 2026 semester for the Interactive Design Practice course that shaped my career aspirations the most. Over the summers I work in construction, building bleachers across the Midwest and finding new ways to make myself a valuable part of a team. The job has allowed me to find creative solutions to problems and get to know the Chicagoland area better, especially through work with diverse clientele.
